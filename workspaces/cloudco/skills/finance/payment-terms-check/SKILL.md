# Payment terms check

> Fictional, for a demo.

## Steps

1. Find the payment terms the customer asks for (Net 30, Net 45, Net 60, …).
2. Compare with our limits (`standard-terms §3`): Net 30 standard; Net 45 Sales; Net 60 Finance decides; beyond Net 60 never.
3. Check the customer's payment history. If they paid late on average this year, recommend against longer terms and propose Net 45. If you don't have the history, say so and ask for it.
4. Cite `standard-terms §3`, `approval-chart §1`/`§2`, any payment record IDs, and this skill's path.
