# Uptime check

> Fictional numbers, for a demo.

## Steps

1. Find the uptime the customer asks for.
2. Compare it with our numbers (`standard-terms §4`):
   - up to 99.9%: standard, OK;
   - above 99.9% and up to 99.95% (our 12-month record): Engineering decides;
   - above 99.95%: never. We can't promise better than we have delivered.
3. If it's "never", recommend offering 99.95% and explain why in one line.
4. Cite `standard-terms §4`, `approval-chart §1`/`§2` and this skill's path.
