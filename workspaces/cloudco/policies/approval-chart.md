# CloudCo approval chart

> Fictional policy for a demo. Read-only: cited by section.

## §1 Who decides

| Change | Who decides |
|---|---|
| Discount up to 15% · payment Net 45 · term up to 3 years | Sales |
| Discount 15–25% · liability cap up to 24 months | CFO |
| Payment Net 60 | Finance |
| Uptime above 99.9%, up to 99.95% | Engineering |

## §2 Never allowed
Nobody can approve: a discount above 25%, payment beyond Net 60, uptime above our 12-month record (99.95%), or unlimited liability.

## §3 Sending to customers
Anything sent to a customer needs a person's approval first. Sales approves sending.
