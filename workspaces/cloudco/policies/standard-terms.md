# CloudCo standard terms

> Fictional policy for a demo. Read-only: cited by section.

## §1 Contract length
Contracts run 1 to 3 years. Sales may agree to any length up to 3 years, with a price lock for that period.

## §2 Pricing and discounts
List prices apply. Sales may give up to 15% off. 15–25% needs the CFO. More than 25% is never allowed.

## §3 Payment terms
Standard payment is Net 30. Sales may agree to Net 45. Net 60 needs Finance, who checks the customer's payment history. Beyond Net 60 is never allowed.

## §4 Service levels
We promise 99.9% monthly uptime. Our measured record over the last 12 months is 99.95%. Engineering may agree to more than 99.9%, up to that record. We never promise more than we have delivered.

## §5 Liability
Our liability is capped at 12 months of fees. The CFO may raise the cap to 24 months. Unlimited liability is never allowed.
